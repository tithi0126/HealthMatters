# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY (IP) OWNERSHIP & ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This Intellectual Property Ownership and Assignment Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of **June 21, 2026** (the "Effective Date"), by and between:

1. **Rahul Sharma**, representing **Health Matters**, having its principal place of business in **Surat, Gujarat, India** (hereinafter referred to as the "Client"); and
2. **Aangan Developers** (hereinafter referred to as the "Developer").

The Client and the Developer are collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party."

This Agreement governs the ownership, licensing, and assignment of all intellectual property rights in connection with the design, development, and deployment of the **Health Matters web platform** (the "Project" or "Platform").

---

## 1. Client Ownership & Deliverables Assignment

### 1.1 Custom Deliverables
Subject to Section 4 of this Agreement (Condition Precedent of Payment), the Developer hereby assigns, transfers, and conveys to the Client all right, title, and interest worldwide in and to the custom deliverables created specifically and exclusively for the Client under the Scope of Work (SOW), including:
- Custom user interface designs, custom CSS styling, and visual layouts;
- Written website copy and copy edits specifically created for the Health Matters platform;
- The custom booking/consultation forms, custom page logic (e.g., self-check assessment widgets), and branding elements developed specifically for this project.

### 1.2 Client-Furnished Materials
The Client retains sole and exclusive ownership of all materials, data, content, information, logos, trademarks, brand names, wellness resources (diet guidelines, counseling philosophies, sound files, etc.) provided to the Developer for incorporation into the Platform ("Client Materials"). The Client grants the Developer a limited, non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide license to use, reproduce, modify, and display the Client Materials solely for the purpose of developing, testing, and supporting the Platform.

---

## 2. Developer Intellectual Property & Tooling

### 2.1 Pre-existing Intellectual Property
The Developer retains sole and exclusive ownership of all rights, title, and interest in and to all pre-existing materials, software, source code, developer libraries, templates, command-line interfaces, utility functions, configuration files, workflows, and database architecture patterns developed prior to or independently of the Project ("Developer IP").

### 2.2 Reusable Components
The Developer routinely uses and develops reusable modules, components, and code structures (such as general React hooks, loading animations, layout systems, navigation components, and utility methods). The Developer retains all ownership of these generic components and may reuse them in other client engagements.

### 2.3 License to Client
To the extent that Developer IP or Reusable Components are embedded in the final Platform, the Developer hereby grants the Client a **non-exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, worldwide, non-transferable** license to use, execute, and display such Developer IP solely as integrated into and running as part of the Platform. The Client shall not extract, reverse-engineer, resell, or distribute the Developer IP independently of the Platform.

---

## 3. Third-Party Materials

The Platform incorporates open-source and third-party software, libraries, and frameworks (including but not limited to **React, Vite, React Router, open-source fonts, and API integration libraries**). 
- The Parties acknowledge that ownership of all third-party materials remains with their respective authors and licensors.
- The Client's use of such third-party materials is governed by their respective licenses (e.g., MIT, Apache, or proprietary API terms of service).
- The Developer is not responsible for any changes in licensing terms or service fees imposed by third-party providers (such as WhatsApp API, cloud email clients, or hosting providers).

---

## 4. Condition Precedent: Timing of IP Transfer

**CRITICAL CLAUSE:** The assignment of intellectual property rights described in Section 1.1 shall only take effect, and ownership shall only pass to the Client, upon the Developer's receipt of **full, final, and cleared payment** of all fees, expenses, and Change Order balances due under the Project payment schedule. 

- Until full payment is received, all intellectual property rights in the Custom Deliverables remain the sole property of the Developer.
- Prior to final payment, the Client is granted a temporary, revocable, non-transferable license to review and test the Platform in a staging environment. Any unauthorized copying, distribution, or public deployment of the code or designs prior to final payment shall constitute a material breach of contract and copyright infringement.

---

## 5. Warranties & Indemnification

### 5.1 Client Warranty
The Client warrants that all Client Materials provided to the Developer do not infringe upon the intellectual property, privacy, or proprietary rights of any third party. The Client agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the Developer against any claims, losses, or legal costs arising from a breach of this warranty.

### 5.2 Developer Warranty
The Developer warrants that the custom code and deliverables created from scratch for the Client are original and do not knowingly infringe the intellectual property rights of any third party. This warranty does not apply to:
- Open-source libraries or third-party packages integrated at the Client's request.
- Modifications made to the code by the Client or any third-party developer post-handover.

---

## 6. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of **India**. Any legal actions, disputes, or proceedings arising out of this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the courts located in **Surat, Gujarat, India**.
