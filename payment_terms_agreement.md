# PAYMENT TERMS AGREEMENT

This Payment Terms Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of **June 21, 2026** (the "Effective Date"), by and between:

1. **Rahul Sharma**, representing **Health Matters** (hereinafter referred to as the "Client"); and
2. **Aangan Developers** (hereinafter referred to as the "Developer").

This Agreement defines the financial terms, payment schedules, and policies regarding invoices, late payment penalties, and project suspension or termination for the Health Matters web platform project.

---

### 1. Project Cost & Milestone Payments
The total project cost is agreed at **INR 23,000 (Twenty-Three Thousand Indian Rupees)**, exclusive of applicable taxes. Payments will be made in installments triggered by the following project milestones:

| Milestone / Trigger | Percentage | Amount (INR) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Milestone 1: Project Kickoff**<br>Triggered upon signing the SOW & NDA (Advance Payment) | 20% | INR 4,600 |
| **Milestone 2: UAT Build Delivery**<br>Triggered upon completion of the core frontend and backend layout and delivery of the private test link (Staging) | 50% | INR 11,500 |
| **Milestone 3: Final Delivery & Launch**<br>Triggered upon live server deployment, domain mapping, and final code sign-off | 30% | INR 6,900 |
| **Total** | **100%** | **INR 23,000** |

---

### 2. Invoicing and Payment Terms
*   **Invoice Issuance:** The Developer will issue an invoice immediately upon reaching each milestone.
*   **Due Date:** All invoices are payable within **seven (7) business days** from the date of invoice issuance.
*   **Payment Methods:** Payments shall be made via Net Banking (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS) or UPI to the designated bank account provided by the Developer.

---

### 3. Late Payment Penalties
To ensure continuous momentum and resource availability for the Project, the Client agrees to the following terms regarding late payments:
*   **Grace Period:** A grace period of three (3) business days from the due date will be allowed.
*   **Interest Charges:** Any payment not received within ten (10) business days from the invoice date will accrue interest at a rate of **2% per month** (compounded monthly) on the outstanding balance, calculated from the original due date until payment is made in full.

---

### 4. Work Suspension for Non-Payment
*   **Suspension Notice:** If any invoice remains unpaid for more than fifteen (15) calendar days from the invoice date, the Developer reserves the right to halt all design, development, and hosting configuration activities.
*   **Suspension Period:** The Developer will give a forty-eight (48) hour warning before active suspension. During a suspension, the Developer is not responsible for any project delays or missed target launch dates.
*   **Reactivation Fee:** To resume work after a suspension due to non-payment, the Client must clear all outstanding invoices, interest penalties, and pay a reactivation fee of **INR 1,000** to realign development resources.

---

### 5. Mid-Project Termination and Non-Payment
In the event that the Client decides to stop the Project mid-way or stops paying altogether:
*   **Payment for Work Done:** The Client is obligated to pay for all work completed up to the date of project termination. The billing will be calculated based on the milestone reached, or on a pro-rata basis for incomplete milestones at a rate of **INR 1,000 per developer hour** of recorded work.
*   **Retention of Intellectual Property:** Until all outstanding invoices are cleared in full, **Aangan Developers retains full ownership of the source code, design layouts, database schemas, and intellectual property** created for the Project. 
*   **Data & Code Deletion:** If payments are not cleared within thirty (30) days of project halting or termination, the Developer reserves the right to delete all staging builds, credentials, and remove temporary server configurations.

---

### 6. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of **India**, and any legal disputes related to billing, payments, and services under this contract will fall under the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of **Surat, Gujarat, India**.

---

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the Parties have executed this Payment Terms Agreement as of the Effective Date.
