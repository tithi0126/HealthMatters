# SCOPE OF WORK (SOW) & DELIVERABLES AGREEMENT

This Scope of Work ("SOW") is entered into and made effective as of **June 21, 2026**, by and between:

1. **Rahul Sharma**, representing **Health Matters** (hereinafter referred to as the "Client"); and
2. **Aangan Developers** (hereinafter referred to as the "Developer").

This document serves as the formal definition of project boundaries, explicit features, and acceptance criteria required for final project sign-off.

---

## 1. Project Objectives
The objective is to design, develop, and launch the web platform for **Health Matters** to promote holistic lifestyle wellness. The site is structured to showcase three core pillars of health:
*   **Diet & Nutrition** (Fat loss, metabolic balance, hormonal support).
*   **Mental Wellbeing** (Stress relief, sound healing, sleep reset).
*   **Yoga & Exercise** (Posture, pain management, desk yoga, strength/mobility for all age groups).

---

## 2. Detailed Scope & Deliverables

The Project consists of the following deliverables to be designed, developed, and deployed:

### A. Core Web Application Pages (Responsive Web Design)
The web application will feature a modern, high-fidelity responsive layout (optimized for Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile devices) containing:
1.  **Home Page:** Hero banner, brand philosophy, pillar summary widgets, statistics, and counselor highlights.
2.  **Pathways Page:** An interactive tabbed or grid interface containing detailed overviews, therapist descriptions, Do's & Don'ts checklists, and symptom indicators for:
    *   *Diet & Nutrition* (Dr. Mala Patel)
    *   *Mental Wellbeing* (Ms. Manvi Shah & Mrs. Anjali Krishnani)
    *   *Yoga & Exercise* (Mr. Rahul Sharma & Mrs. Anjali Krishnani)
3.  **Toolbox (Resource Center):** A page displaying wellness assets, tools, sound meditation guides, and lifestyle tips.
4.  **About Us Page:** Background of Health Matters, the mission statement, values, and credentials of the wellness practitioners.
5.  **Booking / Consultation Page:** A responsive form for users to request free wellness sessions, collecting client name, contact details, selected pillar, and custom message, which will be securely forwarded.

### B. System Integrations
*   **WhatsApp Chat Widget:** A floating button placed across all pages enabling visitors to start a WhatsApp chat with the Health Matters team (+91 72020 15096) or join the community group.
*   **Email Notification System:** Backend integration triggering automatic email notifications to the admin when a new session request is submitted.
*   **SEO Setup:** Page titles, meta tags, Open Graph card tags, and search engine friendly routing.
*   **Web Analytics:** Integration of Google Analytics & Google Search Console tracking scripts.

---

## 3. Explicit Exclusions (Out of Scope)
The following features and resources are **not** included in the current project scope:
*   **User Accounts & Login/Signup:** There will be no customer portals, user registration, database logins, or personal profiles.
*   **Interactive Assessments & Self-Checks:** Interactive calculators or quiz-based diagnosis modules are excluded.
*   **Platform & Hosting Fees:** Domain purchase (`healthmatters.in` or equivalent), cloud/VPS hosting server setup costs, and WhatsApp Business API consumption fees must be paid directly by the client.
*   **Content Writing:** Writing detailed diet sheets, scripts, or recorded sound meditation files (Client will supply all text and audio resources).

---

## 4. Acceptance Criteria for Sign-off
Prior to launch and final payment release, the project must meet the following validation checks:
1.  **Device Compatibility:** Visually appealing and functional across Apple iOS (Safari), Android (Chrome), MacOS, and Windows (Chrome, Safari, Edge).
2.  **Functional Forms:** Verification that the Booking Form submits correctly and triggers the notification system.
3.  **Speed & Performance:** Core Web Vitals loading metrics validated as stable.
4.  **Zero Console Errors:** Clean JavaScript console outputs under standard execution.

---

## 5. Project Authorization & Sign-off

By signing below, both Parties agree to the scope, deliverables, terms, and exclusions outlined in this document. Any modifications to this scope post-signing will require a formal Change Request and separate cost estimation.
