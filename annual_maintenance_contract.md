# ANNUAL MAINTENANCE CONTRACT (AMC)

This Annual Maintenance Contract (the "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of **June 21, 2026** (the "Effective Date"), by and between:

1. **Rahul Sharma**, representing **Health Matters** (hereinafter referred to as the "Client"); and
2. **Aangan Developers** (hereinafter referred to as the "Developer").

---

### 1. Objective & Scope of AMC
The purpose of this Contract is to ensure the continuous operation, security, and stability of the Health Matters web platform after the initial complimentary support period (180 days post-launch) expires. 

This contract is designed to prevent open-ended, free lifetime support, establishing clear parameters for paid maintenance, bug-fixing, and system administration.

---

### 2. Services Covered under AMC

The Developer will provide support and maintenance for the following areas:

*   **A. Bug Fixes & Code Maintenance:**
    *   Resolution of code defects, breaking updates in frontend libraries, or unexpected backend functional failures.
    *   Adjusting code to remain compatible with major browser updates (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox).
*   **B. Database & Server Administration:**
    *   Monitoring and troubleshooting database connectivity and query performance issues.
    *   Addressing database downtime (e.g., MongoDB, Atlas, or relational systems).
    *   Creating and monitoring weekly automated backups of database configurations.
*   **C. Hosting & Deployment Maintenance:**
    *   Managing cloud instance/VPS issues, server restarts, and SSL certificate renewals.
    *   Monitoring server CPU/memory load and fixing performance bottlenecks.
*   **D. Security Patches & Compliance:**
    *   Applying critical security patches to backend frameworks (Node.js/Express, etc.) and npm dependencies.
    *   Updating access tokens, API keys, and environment configurations when needed.

---

### 3. Service Exclusions (Not Covered under AMC)
The AMC does **not** cover the following items, which will be treated as separate custom development work (billed at a standard rate of **INR 1,000 per hour**):
*   Designing or building new pages, subpages, or landing pages.
*   Creating new features, integrations, or workflows (e.g., adding user logins, interactive quizzes, or new booking engines).
*   Migrating to a completely new hosting provider or cloud architecture.
*   Resolving third-party API outages (e.g., if WhatsApp APIs or Email Gateway providers suffer global downtime).
*   Creating marketing banners, promotional assets, or writing content.

---

### 4. Service Level Agreements (SLA) & Response Times
The Developer will address support issues based on severity levels:

| Severity Level | Definition | Response Time | Target Resolution |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Critical** | Site is completely down, server crashed, or database unreachable. | Under 4 Hours | 12 Hours |
| **Major** | A core feature is broken (e.g., Booking Form submissions failing). | Under 12 Hours | 24 Hours |
| **Minor** | Visual alignment issues, broken image links, or minor styling adjustments. | Under 24 Hours | 48 Hours |

---

### 5. Financial Terms & Pricing
*   **AMC Retainer Fee:** Agreed at **25% of the total project build cost**, which translates to **INR 5,750 per year** (payable in advance at the start of the AMC period).
*   **Billing Cycle:** Billed annually. Payment must be cleared within seven (7) business days of invoice.
*   **Complimentary Period:** The AMC will commence immediately after the initial 180-day complimentary post-launch support period ends.
*   **Dedicated Support Hours Limit:** The AMC includes up to **10 hours of minor updates or configurations per year**. Any work exceeding this threshold will be billed at a discounted retainer rate of **INR 800 per hour**.

---

### 6. Termination of Contract
Either party may terminate this agreement by providing **thirty (30) days written notice** to the other party. If terminated by the Client, no refund will be provided for the remaining months of the active annual cycle.

---

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the Parties have executed this Annual Maintenance Contract as of the Effective Date.
