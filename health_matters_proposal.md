## 1. Project Introduction & Overview
This proposal outlines the design, development, and launch of the digital web platform for **Health Matters**. The primary goal of this platform is to establish a modern, engaging, and highly responsive online presence that enables users to access practical wellness guidance and resources built around three core lifestyle pillars:
1. **Diet & Nutrition:** Usable, budget-friendly meal plans and metabolic balance.
2. **Mental Wellbeing:** Short, outcome-focused stress relief and sound healing sessions.
3. **Yoga & Exercise:** Beginner-friendly daily movements, desk yoga, and mobility workouts.

The platform will deliver a smooth, premium user experience optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers.

---

## 2. Scope of Work

### Part 1: Web Application (Frontend + Backend)
A fully customized, high-performance web platform designed with warm, clean, and professional aesthetics tailored to health and holistic wellbeing.
*   **Aesthetics & Theme:** Clean typography, curated calming palettes, smooth scroll animations, and interactive elements.
*   **Core Modules & Pages:**
    *   **Home Page:** High-impact hero section introducing Health Matters, philosophy overview, core statistics, and direct path-selection call-to-actions.
    *   **Pathways Page:** Specialized layout showcasing the three wellness pillars (Diet & Nutrition, Mental Wellbeing, and Yoga & Exercise) along with expert-therapist bios, recommended Do's & Don'ts, and common symptoms addressed.
    *   **Toolbox (Resource Center):** Interactive area highlighting everyday wellness utilities, sound healing exercises, and practical lifestyle resources.
    *   **About Us:** Company vision, mission to simplify health habits, and details of the founding team and counselors.
    *   **Book a Session Page:** A conversion-friendly appointment/consultation booking page with interactive form submission to capture user interests and details.
*   **Domain & Infrastructure Setup Support:**
    *   Technical guidance in registering the domain name, configuring VPS/cloud hosting, and installing SSL Certificates.
*   **Integrations:**
    *   **WhatsApp Community & Chat:** Floating WhatsApp widget and direct community invitation link buttons for instant community engagement.
    *   **Email Notification System:** Integration of automated email alerts for bookings and inquiries (Free).
*   **Search Engine Optimization (SEO) & Analytics:**
    *   Basic SEO tags (Page titles, meta descriptions, alt text, and semantic HTML structure).
    *   Integration with Google Analytics and Google Search Console.
