# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (NDA)

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of **June 21, 2026** (the "Effective Date"), by and between:

1. **Rahul Sharma**, representing **Health Matters** (hereinafter referred to as the "Client", which expression shall include its successors and permitted assigns); and
2. **Aangan Developers** (hereinafter referred to as the "Developer", which expression shall include its partners, employees, and permitted assigns).

The Client and the Developer are collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party."

---

### 1. Purpose
The Parties wish to enter into discussions and collaborate on the development of the digital platform for **Health Matters** (the "Project"). In the course of this Project, the Parties will share proprietary, sensitive, and confidential business, user, and technical data. This Agreement is executed to ensure all such shared information remains strictly confidential and secure.

---

### 2. Definition of Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" refers to any proprietary, non-public data, materials, or information disclosed by one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party"), whether orally, visually, or in writing (including digital transmission), which is marked as confidential or should reasonably be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information.

For the purpose of this Project, Confidential Information explicitly includes, but is not limited to:

#### A. Client's Confidential Information (Protected from Developer's Disclosure)
*   **Business & Company Details:** Operations, strategic partnerships, marketing strategies, and brand expansion plans.
*   **Expert & Personnel Data:** Personal and professional details of therapists, fitness trainers, sound healers, and nutritionists associated with the platform.
*   **Proprietary Content:** Customized diet plans, exercise/yoga routines, sound meditation guides, and lifestyle coaching methodologies.
*   **User & Session Data:** Any contact information, booking logs, consultation details, and clinical or lifestyle metrics submitted by platform users.

#### B. Developer's Confidential Information & IP (Protected from Client's Disclosure)
*   **Development Ideas & Enhancements:** Brainstormed feature ideas, technical suggestions, scalability roadmaps, and future platform enhancements.
*   **Technical Implementation Details:** Unique code libraries, customized modules, database schemas, API architecture, and source code written for the Project.
*   **Critical Technical Data:** Server configurations, hosting credentials, security protocols, and proprietary development workflows.

---

### 3. Obligations of the Parties
Each Party, as a Receiving Party of the other's Confidential Information, agrees to:
1.  **Maintain Strict Confidentiality:** Hold all Confidential Information in the strictest confidence and take all reasonable security measures to prevent unauthorized access, compromise, or disclosure to any third party.
2.  **Restrict Access:** Disclose Confidential Information only to its core team members who have a direct "need-to-know" for the Project execution and who are bound by similar confidentiality obligations.
3.  **No Unauthorized Commercial Use:** Use the Confidential Information solely for the purpose of executing the Project and not for any other commercial, personal, or competitive advantage.
4.  **Secure Storage:** Maintain the security of the source code, user databases, and expert profiles using industry-standard encryption, secure access keys, and standard cybersecurity practices.

---

### 4. Permitted Disclosures
The Receiving Party's obligations under this Agreement shall not apply to information that:
*   Is or becomes publicly known through no fault or breach of the Receiving Party.
*   Was already rightfully in the possession of the Receiving Party prior to disclosure.
*   Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to or reliance on the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.
*   Is required to be disclosed by operation of law, court order, or government regulation, provided the Receiving Party gives prompt notice to the Disclosing Party to seek a protective order.

---

### 5. Term and Termination
*   **Duration:** This Agreement shall govern all disclosures made between the Parties during the course of the Project.
*   **Survival of Obligations:** The obligations of confidentiality, non-compromise of source code, and data security shall survive the completion or termination of the Project for a period of **five (5) years** from the date of project closure, except for proprietary source code and core user data database schemas, which shall remain confidential indefinitely.

---

### 6. Return or Destruction of Data
Upon written request by the Disclosing Party, or upon the termination of this Project, the Receiving Party shall immediately return or securely delete/destroy all physical and digital copies of the Confidential Information in its possession, and provide a written confirmation of such destruction, subject to retaining necessary backups required by developer version control systems (which must remain encrypted and secure).

---

### 7. Remedies for Breach
The Parties acknowledge that any breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages alone would be inadequate. In the event of a breach or threatened breach, the non-breaching Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance, and any other legal remedies, in addition to claiming reasonable damages.

---

### 8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of **India**. Any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts located in **Surat, Gujarat, India**.

---

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the Parties have executed this Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement as of the Effective Date written above.
